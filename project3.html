Visual identity of a contemporary art exhibition.
A comprehensive visual identity project created for an exhibition at Berlin's National Galerie. The design is distinguished by a minimalist yet striking aesthetic that resonates with the character of the museum space. Additionally, a dedicated website and an animation were developed to complement the exhibition's visual identity and extend its presence into the digital space.
