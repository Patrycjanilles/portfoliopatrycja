About
Speakly is an innovative AI-powered tool designed to streamline business meetings. I took on the task of creating a landing page for the product. The page aimed to showcase Speakly’s key features in a simple, engaging, and persuasive way, encouraging users to try the tool. The project was implemented as a single-page application hosted on GitHub Pages, allowing for fast deployment and easy iteration.

Role
UI/UX Designer and Frontend Developer.

Challenge
Design a page that quickly convinces users to download the app, without overwhelming them with content, while providing an intuitive UX.

Logo
For the Speakly project, I designed a minimalist, modern logo that combines simplicity with the dynamic nature of language learning. I chose a fresh blue gradient that evokes associations with technology. The design remains highly legible at small sizes, making the logo versatile.

Wireframing
I started with hand-drawn sketches and then created wireframes in Figma.

Research
Understanding the needs of AI meeting assistant users and analyzing how market leaders communicate value on their landing pages.
